Three ways to begin a world

Pick the kind of space you want to build. Each one starts from a different input and ends in a place you can step into.

Describe it

Type a scene in plain words and watch it assemble around you.

Try: “a glass greenhouse floating above a violet sea at dusk.”

Paint it

Choose a palette and DreamSpace grows terrain, light and weather from it.

Drag one color and the whole sky, fog and shoreline follow.

Hum it

Play or hum a tune and the room moves to its rhythm.

Slow songs make slow tides. Fast ones make storms of light.
